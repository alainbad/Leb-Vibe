## Slide 2 — The Problem

### Lebanese dining culture is world-class. Finding the right place isn't.

- **No single trusted platform** exists to discover restaurants, bars, and nightlife in Lebanon
- People rely on scattered Instagram accounts, WhatsApp groups, and word of mouth
- Restaurant owners have **no digital tools** to manage reservations, reviews, or analytics
- Tourists and expats have **zero reliable discovery layer** when visiting Lebanon
- Lebanon's F&B sector is vibrant and underserved by technology

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

### TableLB is Lebanon's Lifestyle Discovery Platform

A premium, mobile-first platform that connects people with the best places to eat, drink, and experience — and gives venue owners the tools to grow.

**For Users:**
- Discover restaurants, cafés, bars, pubs, beach clubs, and nightlife
- Book tables instantly
- Browse curated collections and live events
- Get AI-powered recommendations

**For Venues:**
- Professional digital profile with gallery, menu, and offers
- Reservation and review management
- Analytics dashboard
- Direct channel to thousands of customers

## Slide 7 — Go-to-Market

### Start with Beirut. Dominate. Then expand.

**Restaurant Acquisition Strategy**

- **Stage 1:** Free onboarding with premium trial — remove all friction to sign up
- **Stage 2:** Demonstrate value through reservations, reviews, and analytics reports
- **Stage 3:** Convert to paid subscriptions with proven ROI

**Consumer Growth Strategy**

- Instagram & TikTok content marketing (food, lifestyle, reviews)
- Influencer and food blogger partnerships
- SEO for search-intent queries ("best sushi Beirut", "rooftop bar Jounieh")
- University campus ambassadors
- PR and editorial features
- Referral program between users

**Launch Timeline**

| Period | Milestone |
|--------|-----------|

| Month 3 | Pilot with 50 curated venues |
| Month 4–6 | Public launch in Beirut |
| Month 7–12 | Nationwide rollout across Lebanon |
| Year 2 | Regional expansion (UAE, KSA, Cyprus) |

## Slide 8 — Competitive Landscape

### No direct competitor owns this space in Lebanon

| Platform | Focus | Lebanon Coverage |
|----------|-------|------------------|
| Zomato / TripAdvisor | Global directories | Generic, not local |
| Toters / Talabat | Food delivery | Not discovery/dine-in |
| Instagram | Social content | No booking, no structure |
| WhatsApp groups | Word of mouth | Zero scale |
| **TableLB** | **Discovery + Booking + Tools** | **Lebanon-first, built for local** |

**Our moat:** Lebanon-specific data, local editorial voice, venue relationships, and a flywheel — more venues attract more users, which attracts more venues.
